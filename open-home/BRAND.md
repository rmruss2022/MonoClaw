# Brand

![openhome](/assets/brand/logo-card.png)

**openhome** is home automation for people who refuse to rent their own homes back from
a cloud. The brand has one job: make *ownership* feel obvious, calm, and cool.

## The idea in one line

> **Your home. Your data. Your keys.**

Alternates:
- *Own your home. Not a subscription to it.*
- *Local-first home automation you actually own.*
- *The smart home that answers to you.*

## The mark

A **house that is also a keyhole**. The house is ownership; the keyhole says *you hold
the key* — to your front door and to your data. It's a single idea a person gets in a
glance, and it reads at favicon size.

- `assets/brand/mark.svg` — icon only (app icon, favicon, avatar)
- `assets/brand/logo.svg` — horizontal lockup (`open` light + `home` mint)
- `assets/brand/favicon.svg` — mark on an ink rounded-square

**Clear space:** keep at least half the mark's width empty around the logo.
**Don't:** recolor the mark outside the palette, add gradients/shadows to it, stretch it,
or put it on busy photos without an ink scrim.

## Voice

Plain, confident, a little rebellious — never corporate, never fear-mongering.

- **Say:** "It runs on your box. Unplug the internet and your home still works."
- **Don't say:** "Leverage our AI-powered smart ecosystem synergies."
- We're pro-ownership, not anti-technology. The tone is *empowering*, not paranoid.
- Lowercase **openhome** in running text. One word.

## Color

Ink and near-black, one confident mint accent. Restraint is the point — it should feel
like well-made hardware, not a toy.

| Token | Hex | Use |
|-------|-----|-----|
| Ink | `#0e1014` | primary background |
| Panel | `#171a21` | cards, surfaces |
| Line | `#272c37` | borders, dividers |
| Mint (primary) | `#3ddc97` | the accent — logo, highlights, actions |
| Mint deep | `#1f7a56` | active borders, pressed states |
| Text | `#e7ebf3` | primary text |
| Muted | `#8b93a7` | secondary text |
| Signal amber | `#f0b429` | warnings only |
| Signal red | `#ef5350` | danger / offline only |

Mint is precious — use it for *one* thing per view. If everything is mint, nothing is.

## Type

System sans (SF / Segoe / Roboto) for product UI — fast, native, no web-font tax, and it
matches the local-first ethos. The wordmark is lowercase, tight-tracked. `open` in text
weight, **home** in bold.

## What the brand promises

1. **It works offline.** Local-first isn't a feature, it's the whole point.
2. **You own the data.** It lives on your disk. You can export or delete it, always.
3. **Open protocols only.** No device here is a hostage to a vendor cloud.
4. **No subscription to function.** Buy the box (or build it) and it's yours.

Everything we design should pass one test: *does this make the person feel more in
control of their home, or less?*
