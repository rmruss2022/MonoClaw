How it works

Your hub is the only thing that touches your home

Install it on your own box. It finds your devices on the local network and listens for your voice. A thin cloud only helps the app find your hub — house data never flows through it.

Run it on your box

Clone the repo onto a Pi or an old laptop. It joins your Wi-Fi and discovers your Matter, ESPHome & Zigbee devices. No accounts needed.

Say “Hey Jarvis”

Speak naturally. The hub recognizes you on-device and acts instantly — lights, music, scenes, questions.

Keep your keys

Everything stays local-first and open-source. Your data lives on your disk; you can read the code, export, or wipe it anytime.
