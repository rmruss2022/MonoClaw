How it works

Your hub is the only thing that touches your home

A thin cloud routes the app to your hub with a short-lived key. House data never flows through us.

Plug in the hub

A small box joins your Wi-Fi and finds your devices on the local network. No accounts needed to run.

Say “Hey Jarvis”

Speak naturally. The hub recognizes you on-device and acts instantly — lights, music, scenes, questions.

Keep your keys

Everything stays local-first and token-authed. Your data lives on your disk; you can export or wipe it anytime.
