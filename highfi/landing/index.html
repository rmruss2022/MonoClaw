How it works

Three moves. One pocket.

  1. 1

    Screw in your cart

    Spring-loaded 510 connector on top. Thread in any standard cart and you're locked and loaded.

  2. 2

    Load your tracks

    Drop MP3s on a microSD card or upload over WiFi from the app. Your library, stored locally.

  3. 3

    Press play & rip

    Onboard speaker fires up. Fire button vapes, play button plays — one-handed, one session.

Everything on board

Audio, vape, and control in a single 18650-class body.

Onboard speaker

A MAX98357A class-D amp drives a 3 W micro speaker. No earbuds required — the room hears it.

MP3 & AAC playback

Play MP3, AAC, WAV, and FLAC straight off microSD — or upload tracks over WiFi from the app.

Phone app remote

The controller

Your phone is the remote

HighFi serves its own mobile-first web app over WiFi — no download, no account. Connect and you get the whole session in one place.

  • Now playing — transport, seek, volume, shuffle and repeat.
  • Library — browse tracks on the card and upload new ones over WiFi.
  • Vape panel — battery, coil status, wattage and session timer, clearly separated from music.
  • Vibe & LED — set the RGB mood with presets like "sunset".

Develops fully against a built-in emulator — zero hardware required.

FAQ

Does it stream to Bluetooth speakers?

The onboard speaker is the primary output. HighFi runs on an ESP32-S3, which is BLE-only — it doesn't do Classic Bluetooth (A2DP), so it can't stream to standard BT speakers or headphones today. Bluetooth-out is a planned option that would need a dedicated BT-transmitter module. Documented, not faked.

Is this a certified consumer product?

No. HighFi is an experimental maker reference design. It is not FDA, UL, or CE certified and is not for sale as-is. It's published so makers can learn from and build on it.

Who is it for, and what can I put in it?

Adults of legal age only, using legal carts where permitted. Cannabis hardware is state-regulated and nicotine devices have their own requirements — know and follow your local law before building or using one.

Is the vape side actually safe?

A heating element plus a Li-ion cell is not a toy. Every fire path has interlocks enforced in firmware: a 10-second hard cutoff, short-circuit and no-atomizer lockouts, over-temperature cutoff, battery protection, and a 5-click lock. Setpoints are clamped in firmware — the app is treated as untrusted input.

Do I need an account or the cloud?

No. HighFi is local-first. It hosts its own control app over WiFi and keeps your music library on the microSD card. No login, no cloud dependency.

How do I get music onto it?

Copy MP3/AAC/WAV/FLAC files onto a microSD card, or upload tracks over WiFi from the app. A few tracks can also live in on-device flash.
