## Changes
- **Account and email fixes:** added a forgot-password flow. It emails a 6-digit code, stores only a hash of it, expires it after 15 minutes, allows 5 tries, sends at most one code per minute, and signs out other sessions after a reset. Email sending was fixed by giving Resend requests a real User-Agent. Signup now blocks mistyped email domains, and an admin `fixemail` action was added. The temporary mail-debug endpoint was added, used and then removed. Enter now submits the login and reset screens.
- **Onboarding rebuilt:** animated scenes, more questions, and signup moved to the end.
- **Home screen overhaul:** new header. Today's pick was decluttered and now follows journey order and skips completed lessons. New Quick meditate card with a session bottom sheet and the chosen soundscape icon. Atlas became a color-coded "map of your practice" with a progress bar per dimension. The horoscope was redesigned without emoji glyphs. Home sections were reordered, and toasts now sit below the Dynamic Island.
- **Journal:** the call-to-action button went through about 10 styling rounds and ended inline: tome icon, text, and a seal tilted 45°, with softer sparks. The Journal home has a new mandala-inspired hero. Deep, Daily and Season each got a color and a custom icon (compass rose, open book, veined leaf).
- **Pillars and Jungian path:** the focus display case now sits in an arched niche (gold arch outline plus fluted pilasters) instead of the column Lottie, which was dropped. There are new pillar cards, sparks on the featured journal, and a first scaffold of the Jungian path.
- **Journeys and Collect:** Journey screens were brought up to the home-screen standard. The lesson preview is now a bottom sheet sized with `dvh` and safe-area padding to fix its height on iOS. Continue and pledge buttons are now luxe gold. The Collect mandala was replaced by the ornate Wholeness mandala (`assets/mandala.html`), and the service worker no longer serves a stale copy of it.
- **Leagues, map and loader:** leagues now use two-color gradients with the 7 colors evenly spaced, and the league card is slimmer. Africa seed data was removed, and `admin/africa?do=recompute` rebuilds real users' stats. The diya flame Lottie is now the loader on boot and everywhere else.

## Suggestions for tomorrow
1. **Finish the Jungian path.** It is only a scaffold. Define the stages, what unlocks each one (journal entries, lessons) and how progress is stored, then link it to the Pillars display case so it isn't an empty placeholder.
2. **Test the forgot-password flow end to end on a real phone.** Check that the code arrives through Resend and that a wrong, expired or 5-tries-used code shows the right message. Also check that other devices are signed out after a reset. Note that `SESSIONS` lives only in memory; if sessions are also saved somewhere, those need clearing too.
3. **Add a per-IP limit to `/api/forgot` and `/api/reset`.** Today the only limit is per email. Without an IP limit, one client can request codes for many addresses (Resend cost and spam) or keep guessing codes.
4. **Bump the service worker cache in a release checklist.** Today's stale mandala showed that cached assets can outlive deploys. Add an asset version (a hash or the mtime of `assets/*`) to the `CACHE` name automatically instead of bumping `lumen-v2` by hand.
5. **Check the new screens on the smallest iPhone.** That means onboarding, the lesson bottom sheet, the Pillars arch and the Journal hero. Look for overflow, safe-area problems and the frame rate with sparks, the mandala and the diya all animating.
6. **Settle the journal CTA design.** It changed about 10 times today. Pick the final version and remove leftover styles and unused icon definitions (the earlier tome and manuscript icons) from `server.py`.
7. **Run `do=recompute` once on prod** and spot-check a few real users' streaks and minutes after the Africa cleanup. Then retire the `admin/africa` endpoints, or protect them behind an admin check.
8. **Start splitting up `lumen/server.py` (8,900 lines).** Nearly every commit edits this one file. Moving the main front-end JS and CSS into `assets/` would make diffs and reviews much easier.
