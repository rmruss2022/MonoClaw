## Changes
- **First-party product analytics (`1580b28`):** a new `events` table with 3 indexes. A batched client tracker (`track()`, sent with `sendBeacon` or `keepalive` fetch, keyed by a `lumen_aid` anon id) fires `screen_view` on every `go(tab)` and auth screen, and `onboarding_step` on each onboarding slide. There's an unauthenticated `POST /api/ev` ingest capped at 25 events per batch, plus `admin_analytics()` behind the owner-only `GET /api/admin/analytics?days=` (onboarding funnel with conversion %, top 25 screens, 14-day DAU, visitors/signups/events). A new Analytics tab in `/admin` shows it.
- **Login 502 fixed (`9c43212`):** a local `import hmac, hashlib` inside the SMS webhook branch made those names local to all of `_post_api`, so login crashed with UnboundLocalError. I checked: no other indented imports of module-global names are left in `server.py`.
- **TTS consolidation:** horoscope and daily-pick narration now use the lesson ElevenLabs pipeline (`475be3d`). One ElevenLabs failure no longer kills the voice for the whole session (`43fabc2`). The browser `speechSynthesis` "robot voice" fallback is removed, so it's ElevenLabs or silence (`4628362`).
- **Orb and boot polish:** the orb glow now grows as you press and spin it (`ac5433b`, `orb.js` plus the cache-bust in `server.py`). The web boot loader diya is resized to match the native splash (`5e24c86`, +91 lines of inline loader markup and CSS).
- **Last night's sweep (`ead6f0a`):** SMS streak-risk now only fires when `last == yesterday`, and inbound STOP/START matching tolerates punctuation.
- **Working tree:** still mostly noise (staged inbound media, TTS mp3s, sweep logs, reports, portal JSON). `lumen/appstore/screenshots-v2/` and `media/baby_cobra_schedule.md` are still uncommitted.
