# Night Sweep — 2026-11-09

## Changes
- **SMS notifications via Twilio (new):** phone + consent captured at signup, an `sms_optout` flag, and a sparse lifecycle engine (`_sms_candidate` / `sms_tick`). It sends two kinds of text: an evening streak-at-risk nudge (19:00 local) and a weekly win-back for dormant or churned users. There's an `SMS_MODE` switch (off/dryrun/live) and a `sms_log` table that prevents duplicate texts.
- **SMS hardening:** the `/api/sms/inbound` webhook now checks `X-Twilio-Signature` with HMAC-SHA1 and keeps STOP/START in sync with Twilio. Users can now manage SMS in the app through `/api/sms/prefs` (Profile toggle plus phone field). There's an owner-only `/api/admin/sms/test` and a new `lumen/SMS_SETUP.md` guide.
- **Lifecycle email engine + premium conversion funnel** (+452 lines in `server.py`): the biggest backend change of the day.
- **New-user feedback survey at `/survey`:** it shows one question per screen, with questions tailored to each module and multi-select benefit questions. The Mandala module and map question wording were tuned.
- **Home orb:** you can drag it to rotate in 3D with flick inertia. The `uSpin` uniform now rotates the lit face, plasma, motes and comet trails, so the orb stays where you leave it. The canvas sizes itself with a ResizeObserver. `orb.js?v=3` plus several SW cache bumps (now `lumen-v17`) get past stale service-worker caches.
- **Journal/Pillars:** the Pillars display case merged into Journal home (3 entry doors, stone columns, "More pillars"). Added bespoke Jungian archetype icons and renamed it to "The Jungian Journal". Tapping a pillar now opens its full chapter list (new `/api/pillar` endpoint).
- **Mandala screen ("Your Mandala"):** it renders full length with whole-page scroll, and the iframe sizes itself via postMessage. The sticky panel nav is solid, the Artifacts toggle moved to the page bottom, and there's more top padding.
- **Engagement polish:** journeys are sorted by most recently practiced (that one pulses), Map/Leagues/Profile were redesigned (premium league emblem, gold brand), meditations can be shared from the reward screen, and session/lesson screens are centered.
