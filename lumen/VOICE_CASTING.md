# Lumen — Voice Narrator Casting / Audition Pack

Guided-meditation narration for **Lumen**, a meditation app. We're casting a
calm, grounded voice to narrate our "deity" courses — short guided sits that
teach through Hindu archetypes (Vishnu, Shiva, Lakshmi). Ongoing work: dozens of
3–8 minute scripts.

## The voice we're after
- **Tone:** warm, intimate, unhurried. Like being guided by someone wise and kind
  who is sitting right next to you — not a radio announcer, not a yoga cliché.
- **Pace:** slow. Room to breathe. Comfortable with silence between lines.
- **Feel:** grounded, sincere, a little reverent — never sleepy, never "wellness
  salesman." Think late-night, close-mic, lamplit.
- **Range/accent:** open to any gender. Neutral or lightly textured accent is fine.
  Must handle Sanskrit terms gracefully (guide below) — this is the #1 thing.

## Audition side A — "The Preserver" (primary; ~75 sec)
Read slowly, warmly. This is the opening of a sit; you're inviting the listener in.

> Come and take the seat of Vishnu, the Preserver — the one who holds the whole
> world in balance, not by force but by steady presence. In the great trinity he
> stands between Brahma, who creates, and Shiva, who dissolves; his is the quiet,
> unglamorous middle — keeping, tending, sustaining. His quality is sattva:
> clarity, harmony, an even keel.
>
> So let the body grow still, and begin to notice the breath that has kept you
> alive all this time — quietly, faithfully, asking nothing of you. That breath is
> already his work in you.

## Audition side B — "The Ocean Breath" (optional contrast; ~45 sec)
Shows instructional delivery — guiding a technique without losing the calm.

> Narrow the back of the throat just slightly, as if to fog a mirror with the
> mouth closed, until the breath hushes like waves drawing back over sand. That
> soft sound gives the mind a single thread to follow. It is said to echo the
> shankha, Vishnu's conch — the first sound, from which all worlds arose. So ride
> the quiet roar of your own breathing, and let it hold you the way the ocean
> holds the sleeping god.

## Sanskrit pronunciation guide
- **Vishnu** — VISH-noo
- **sattva** — SUT-vuh
- **Brahma** — BRUH-mah
- **Shiva** — SHEE-vuh
- **shankha** (conch) — SHUNG-kuh
- **Ujjayi** (ocean breath) — oo-JAH-yee
- **dharma** — DHAR-muh
- **Narayana** — nah-RAH-yuh-nuh
- **Om Namo Narayanaya** — OHM NAH-moh nah-RAH-yuh-NAH-yuh
- **Kailash** — KYE-lahsh
> When unsure, slow down and land each syllable cleanly rather than rushing past it.

## Technical / delivery
- **Format:** WAV, 48kHz/24-bit (or 44.1kHz/16-bit), mono.
- **Sound:** dry, close, treated room. Minimal reverb, low noise floor. No music —
  we add the soundscape.
- **Pacing:** leave natural pauses; we'll edit. Don't fill silence.
- **Audition:** record **Side A** (Side B a plus). Phone-quality demo is fine for
  the audition; final work must be studio-clean.
