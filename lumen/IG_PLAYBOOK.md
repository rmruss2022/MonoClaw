# Lumen — Instagram Playbook

## Hero concept: "Light up the map, one real place at a time"
Lumen's core mechanic = *every sit lights up a living world map.* The founder
(Matthew) meditating in new local spots dramatizes that literally: each new
location = a new pin lit. It also sells the deeper promise — **get out of the
house, meditate anywhere, your inner world and the real map both grow.** One
throughline carries the whole account.

## Content pillars (rotate)
1. **The Sit (hero, ~50%)** — you meditating in a new local spot. The "getting
   out of the house" series. Each ends with the app's map lighting that pin.
2. **The Teaching (~20%)** — one idea from a deity/journey lesson, 20–40s, your
   voice or text-over-b-roll. Repurpose the narration scripts you already have.
3. **The Product (~20%)** — screen-recordings: the orb, the world map lighting
   up, a journey, the 5-year-plan journal. Satisfying, quiet, no hard sell.
4. **The Human (~10%)** — why you built it, build-in-public, behind the scenes.

## The Sit — reusable Reel formula (your bread & butter)
- **0–2s hook (text on screen):** pick one —
  - "POV: you stopped meditating in your bedroom."
  - "Day 7 of meditating somewhere new until Lumen hits #1."
  - "Nobody meditates *here*." (then you do)
- **2–15s:** you walk in, settle, sit. Natural sound. One slow push-in or static tripod.
- **15–25s:** calm text-over — one line of the teaching, or what this place taught you.
- **25–30s (payoff):** cut to the app — this location's pin lights up on the map.
- **Caption:** 1 short story sentence + 1 teaching line + CTA ("Comment a spot I should sit next"). 
- **Audio:** trending calm/ambient sound (boosts reach) OR your own Lumen narration.

## Cadence
- **4–5 posts/week.** ~3 Reels (Sits), 1 product, 1 teaching/human.
- Reels are the growth engine on IG right now — favor them hard over static.
- Best windows (ET): 6–8am, 12–1pm, 7–9pm. Test and let insights decide.
- **Stories daily** (low effort): behind-the-scenes of the shoot, polls
  ("next sit: woods or water?"), repost the day's teaching.

## Shoot kit (keep it dead simple so you actually do it)
- Phone + a small flexible tripod (Joby-style) + optional lav mic for the teaching bits.
- Shoot vertical 4K. Golden hour (first/last hour of light) makes everything look good.
- Batch: film 3–4 Sits in one outing at different spots → a week of content.
- B-roll each spot: wide establishing, feet/hands settling, eyes-closed close-up,
  the surroundings (water, leaves, skyline). 10s each — gives editing options.

## 30-day arc
- **Wk 1:** establish the concept + face. The manifesto + first 3 Sits + 1 product.
- **Wk 2:** widen locations, start the teachings, first "comment where I sit next."
- **Wk 3:** lean into whichever Sit performed best; invite others ("send me your spot").
- **Wk 4:** first soft conversion push — a Reel that ends on the app + "link in bio,"
  and recruit 2–3 creators (the voice/creator-discovery pipeline) to do their own Sit.

## Metrics that matter (first 60 days)
- Saves + shares > likes (IG ranks on them). Watch-through on Reels > 50%.
- Profile visits → link taps (the real funnel). Follower growth is vanity by comparison.
- Tie back to Lumen's own analytics (the admin funnel) to see IG → installs → onboarding.
